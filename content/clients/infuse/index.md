Infuse is a polished, closed-source video player from Firecore for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Mac. It connects directly to Jellyfin, Plex, and Emby libraries (alongside NAS shares, SMB/WebDAV sources, and cloud storage), pulling in artwork and metadata automatically without any file conversion or transcoding for most formats.

Playback covers a wide range of video, audio, and subtitle formats, with hardware-accelerated 4K HDR, HDR10+, and Dolby Vision, plus Dolby and DTS audio passthrough. AirPlay and Google Cast streaming, two-way Trakt sync for watched history and ratings, and one-click OpenSubtitles downloads round out the free tier. Infuse Pro, available as a subscription or one-time lifetime purchase, unlocks additional formats, cloud storage sources, video trailers, and cross-device library and playback sync.
