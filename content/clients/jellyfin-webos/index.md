Jellyfin for webOS is the official app for LG smart TVs. It's a small native wrapper around the same [Jellyfin Web](https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin-web) client that runs in a browser or ships with every server, so most development and features happen there and land on webOS automatically, while the wrapper handles remote input, TV-specific playback, and installation through LG's Content Store.
