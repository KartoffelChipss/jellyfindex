Catppuccin for Jellyfin brings the community-favorite soothing pastel palette to Jellyfin's web client, with four flavors to choose from: Latte, Frappé, Macchiato, and Mocha.

The accent color can be overridden after the theme and flavor imports, and it's part of the wider [Catppuccin](https://catppuccin.com) ecosystem of ports for dozens of apps.
