ElegantFin is a Jellyfin theme inspired by Jellyseerr's design that gives the web client a fresh, modern look with a single import. It adds new layouts and color tones, smoother animations, rounded corners and consistent spacing, and stylish borders, hover effects, and shadows across the interface, while cutting down on unnecessary clutter.

The theme is built to look the same whether you're on phone, desktop, or TV, and it ships with a handful of optional customizations: custom media covers for libraries, a custom login page background, extra overlay buttons on hover, and a centered play button on cards.

You can find instructions on how to customize the theme in the [README](https://github.com/lscambo13/ElegantFin/tree/main#-how-to-customise-this-theme).
