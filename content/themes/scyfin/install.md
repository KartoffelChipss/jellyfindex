**Jellyfin 10.11.x and above:**

```css
@import url('https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/loof2736/scyfin@latest/CSS/scyfin-theme.css');
```

**Jellyfin 10.10.x and below:** replace `@latest` with `@1.4` in the URL above (and in any options you use).

Paste into a Custom CSS box. Optional add-ons and accent-color themes (Seafoam, Coral, Snow, and more) are listed in the [README](https://github.com/loof2736/scyfin#readme).
