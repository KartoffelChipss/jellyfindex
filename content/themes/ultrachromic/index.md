Ultrachromic is the latest evolution of CTalvio's "chromic" theme series for Jellyfin, offered as several presets: Monochromic, Kaleidochromic, and Novachromic.

Novachromic and Kaleidochromic support custom accent colors, and additional fixes and font stylesheets can be layered on top. Note: the theme may not work correctly behind a reverse proxy. See the [README](https://github.com/CTalvio/Ultrachromic#readme) for details and the full list of module combinations.
